Positioning Concepts

1. Position Types: The position property controls how elements are placed on a webpage.

2. Position Values: Top, bottom, left and right control the element's location.

3. Z-Index: Controls the stacking order of overlapping elements.

4. Responsive Quadrant Layout: Divides the business display into four sections and adjusts to different screen sizes.
